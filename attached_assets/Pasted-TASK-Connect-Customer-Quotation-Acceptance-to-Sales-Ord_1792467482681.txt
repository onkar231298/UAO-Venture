TASK: Connect Customer Quotation Acceptance to Sales Order Creation

Current application state

The following features are already implemented and must remain unchanged:

* Customer management.
* Sales representative dashboard.
* Customer dashboard.
* Sales representative can generate a quotation.
* The quotation can be sent or made available to the customer through the existing dashboard workflow.
* The customer can download the quotation as a PDF.

Email integration will be implemented later. Do not work on email integration in this task.

We currently have one dummy customer and one dummy sales representative. Use these existing accounts only.

Required workflow

1. The sales representative creates a quotation using the existing functionality.
2. The customer opens the quotation from the customer dashboard.
3. The customer can accept or reject the quotation directly from the dashboard.
4. If accepted, the system records the acceptance and creates a linked Sales Order.
5. If rejected, the system records the rejection and reason, if provided. It must not create a Sales Order.
6. The sales representative can see the quotation’s updated status and the resulting Sales Order, if one was created.

Business rules

* Preserve the existing quotation PDF generation and download functionality.
* Do not require email integration for acceptance or rejection.
* The customer can respond only to quotations belonging to that customer.
* The customer must not be able to modify the quotation’s prices, products, quantities, taxes or commercial terms while accepting or rejecting it.
* Record the response, timestamp and customer account responsible for the action.
* Acceptance must be processed securely on the backend.
* Only one Sales Order may be created from a given accepted quotation.
* Repeated clicks, page refreshes or simultaneous requests must not create duplicate Sales Orders.
* The Sales Order must retain a link to the original quotation and customer.
* Copy the accepted quotation’s products, quantities, prices, currency, taxes, delivery terms and payment terms accurately.
* Do not silently modify the original quotation.
* Rejection must not create an order.
* If a quotation has expired, been cancelled or is otherwise ineligible, block acceptance according to the application’s rules.
* If acceptance tracking or Sales Order generation already partially exists, reuse and complete it instead of creating duplicate tables or workflows.

Scope restrictions

Do not create additional customers or users.
Do not rebuild the dashboards.
Do not modify quotation PDF generation.
Do not implement email integration.
Do not create purchase orders, inventory transactions, production orders, supplier payments or bank transactions.
Do not modify unrelated CRM modules.

Testing

Using the existing dummy customer and sales representative:

1. Create or open an eligible quotation.
2. Accept it from the customer dashboard.
3. Confirm the acceptance is saved.
4. Confirm exactly one linked Sales Order is created.
5. Verify the order details against the original quotation.
6. Confirm the sales representative can view the updated quotation status and Sales Order.
7. Test rejection and confirm no Sales Order is created.
8. Test repeated acceptance and page refresh to confirm duplicate orders are prevented.
9. Verify customer permissions prevent access to another customer’s quotation.
10. Confirm the existing PDF download and dashboard functionality still work.

Report the actual test results and any remaining issues. Make only the minimum changes required for this task. Stop after completion and wait for the next instruction.
