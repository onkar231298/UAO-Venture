 Major Modules Overview for the Platform:
🧠 1. Website Management (Backend)
Upload/manage products shown on the public-facing website.

Control product categories, product URLs, SEO metadata, and structured data.

Upload certificates, stock status, pricing, and product specifications in HTML.

Manage homepage sliders, banners, and featured sections.

Auto-generate product IDs used in CRM, Inventory, PO/SO.

Controls product visibility on the website.

Updates product display like MOQ, Packaging Size, HS Code, etc.

👤 2. User Management
Create and manage internal roles: Super Admin, Admin, Sales Manager, Sales Executive, Purchase Manager, Logistics Manager, etc.

Each department has Manager + Executive hierarchy.

Role-based access controls.

Customer users can sign up from the frontend (buyers).

🧾 3. Sales Management & CRM
Lead tracking, enquiry management, customer interactions.

Create sales quotations, convert to sales orders.

Auto-track product-wise leads.

Auto-capture customer interactions.

Assign leads to sales team members.

📥 4. Purchase Management
Raise purchase requests and generate purchase orders.

Link POs to products from website management (using product ID).

Manage vendor details and PO history.

📦 5. Inventory Management
Track available stock for each product.

Show current stock on the website (optional).

Logistic team can dispatch product from inventory.

Auto-update stock post dispatch.

🚚 6. Logistics Management
View inventory details.

Create packing list for dispatch.

Once packing list is created, stock reduces.

Update dispatch status (in transit, delivered, etc.)

🔍 7. SEO & Structured Data
Add meta title, meta description for each product/page.

Add OpenGraph and schema.org structured data.

Pages should pass all Google structured data tests.

🧑‍💻 8. Admin Dashboard
Unified dashboard for all departments.

Analytics widgets: Total Leads, Total Products, Inventory Value, Orders, Dispatch, Inquiries, etc.

Filter by time, product, country.
