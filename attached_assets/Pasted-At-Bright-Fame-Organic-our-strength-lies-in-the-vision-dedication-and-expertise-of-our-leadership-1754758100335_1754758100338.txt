At Bright Fame Organic, our strength lies in the vision, dedication, and expertise of our leadership team. Each member brings a unique set of skills and years of industry experience, driving our mission to deliver the highest-quality organic and natural ingredients worldwide.

Dr. Niraj Patel – Managing Director
A young, dynamic, and self-made entrepreneur, Dr. Niraj Patel has built Bright Fame Organic from the ground up without any family business background. With an MS in Orthopedics, he made an inspiring transition into the food and spice industry in 1998, eventually becoming one of the most recognized figures in the sector. Known for his calm demeanor and strategic mindset, Dr. Niraj is often called “the man with future eyes” at Bright Fame Organic, guiding the company with innovation, foresight, and a passion for excellence.

Ujjwal Dadhich – Director of Purchase
With an extensive career in the Indian food and ingredients industry, Ujjwal Dadhich has worked with some of the country’s most renowned companies before joining Bright Fame Organic. As Director of Purchase, he brings unmatched sourcing expertise and strong relationships across APMC markets and organic farming groups nationwide. Respected for his quiet leadership style and innovative ideas, Ujjwal plays a pivotal role in ensuring our supply chain remains strong, ethical, and efficient.

Onkar Suryawanshi – Director of Operations
A qualified Food Technologist and technology enthusiast, Onkar Suryawanshi began his career at a young age, driven by a deep passion for food safety, certifications, and advanced processing technologies. As President of the NGO GSRL Foundation, he works to bridge the gap between industry and education, mentoring students in Food Technology and Dairy Technology. He is also actively involved in training farmers in organic agriculture practices. Onkar has published multiple papers in international journals and was honored with the Young Achiever Award 2020 by the Indian Achievers Forum. His operational expertise ensures Bright Fame Organic consistently delivers on its promise of quality and innovation.
