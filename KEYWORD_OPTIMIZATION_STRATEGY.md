## Technical SEO Optimizations

### 1. Title Tag Improvements
**Current:** Product Name
**Optimized Format:** "[Primary Keyword] | [Secondary Keyword] | BFI Organic"

Examples:
- "Organic Amla Powder | Indian Gooseberry Extract | BFI Organic"
- "Haritaki Powder | Terminalia Chebula | Ayurvedic Ingredients | BFI Organic"

### 2. Meta Description Optimization
**Format:** [Primary keyword] supplier. [Key benefits]. [Certifications]. [Geographic targeting]. Get quotes for bulk [product].

Example:
"Premium organic amla powder supplier. Rich in vitamin C & antioxidants. USDA & EU organic certified. Bulk quantities available for Europe, US & Canada. Get competitive quotes today."

### 3. Header Structure (H1-H6)
```
H1: [Primary Keyword] - [Product Name]
H2: Key Benefits of [Product]
H2: [Product] Specifications
H3: Organic Certifications
H3: Applications & Uses
H3: Geographic Availability
H2: Why Choose BFI Organic for [Product]?
```

### 4. Content Optimization

3. **Add industry-specific terms** (nutraceutical, pharmaceutical, food grade)
