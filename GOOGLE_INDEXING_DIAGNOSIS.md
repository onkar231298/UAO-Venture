# Google Indexing Issues Diagnosis & Solutions

## Current Status: 164 Discovered Pages, Not Indexed

Based on Google Search Console best practices and research, here are the specific reasons your pages aren't being indexed and the immediate solutions:

## **Root Causes Identified**

### 1. **New Website/Domain Issue** 
**Problem**: Google takes 1-4 weeks to index new websites or newly created pages
**Evidence**: Your industry landing pages were just created (January 21, 2025)
**Solution**: This is normal - Google needs time to discover and evaluate new content

### 2. **Content Quality Concerns**
**Problem**: Google may perceive content as lacking unique value or thin
**Evidence**: Your pages may be competing with established competitors who have more authority
**Solution**: Enhance content depth and add unique industry insights

### 3. **Internal Linking Structure**
**Problem**: New pages lack sufficient internal links from established pages
**Evidence**: Your industry pages (/food-ingredients, /organic-herbs, /eu-organic-spices) need more internal connections
**Solution**: Add strategic internal links from high-authority pages

### 4. **Server Performance Issues**
**Problem**: Slow response times can cause Google to defer indexing
**Evidence**: Need to test server response times and Core Web Vitals
**Solution**: Optimize server performance and page load speeds

### 5. **Crawl Budget Allocation**
**Problem**: Google allocates limited crawl budget, prioritizing most important pages first
**Evidence**: 164+ pages discovered but Google prioritizing established content
**Solution**: Optimize crawl budget by improving page priority signals
