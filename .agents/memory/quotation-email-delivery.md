---
name: Quotation email delivery
description: The current scope and sender identity for customer quotation emails.
---

Do not send quotation emails yet. Publishing a quote should make it available in the customer dashboard and as a downloadable PDF. When transactional email is implemented later, send from the assigned salesperson's G Suite account.

**Why:** The owner explicitly deferred quotation email delivery and specified the salesperson's own G Suite address as the future sender.

**How to apply:** Keep quote creation and dashboard publishing separate from email. Do not add or activate email sending unless the owner requests that follow-up.
