---
name: All-user interface redesign scope
description: Owner's scope and option-preservation requirement for the approved workspace redesign.
---

The owner wants all users' signed-in interfaces in the same style, not only Admin and Sales. Their most important requirement is: "dont miss any avalble option while doing this".

**Why:** the owner explicitly expanded the proposed redesign and prioritized preserving available options.

**How to apply:** use consistent design and placement across roles while retaining existing permissions. Inventory all menus, conditional actions, fields, tabs, dialogs, and module options before visual changes, and reconcile them afterward; a missing option prevents completion.

The owner rejected colour/font/theme-only changes as insufficient. They want the reference video's layout considered, and explicitly do not want duplicate company/contact search and Create controls beside the theme toggle when those actions already exist.

**Why:** the owner corrected the delivered redesign and supplied the video again.

**How to apply:** improve layout and hierarchy rather than merely recolouring existing screens; keep existing actions available without adding redundant toolbar copies. Use their supplied palette: navy #172B4D sidebar/header, warm white #F7F7F2 background, amber #D5851F primary actions, sage #87A96B success, white #FFFFFF cards/tables, muted red #C94C4C urgent states.

The owner approved the revised compact-navigation layout across all users, with the clarification: "this looks okay but dont change any alredy avaliable".

**Why:** approval is for layout placement and visual hierarchy, not replacing business workflows or reducing role-specific choices.

**How to apply:** retain original forms, actions, fields, role restrictions and full navigation access while applying the approved structure; do not treat the mockup's fictional records as application data.

The owner likes the short company details in the right-side people desk, but prioritizes efficient space use and wants the left sidebar expandable.

**Why:** the owner explicitly liked the desk while correcting poor space management and its first-company-only behavior.

**How to apply:** retain the compact company/people context as a design direction, allow selecting other companies without requiring full details, and keep navigation and detail panels adjustable so they do not unnecessarily reduce the records area.

The owner approved explicit parent/child navigation: parent groups and sections with sub-options need disclosure arrows and indented children, consistently in expanded, compact and mobile menus.

**Why:** the owner could not readily tell that Sales Management has its own sub-options within Operations.

**How to apply:** keep page-label navigation separate from submenu disclosure, show children under their existing parent, reveal active-page ancestors, and do not flatten the hierarchy or invent new permissions.

CRM colour themes are personal settings, including for the Sales team, not a
company-wide branding control.

**Why:** the owner requested rich, focused backend colour combinations and said
Sales staff should be able to change their own theme.

**How to apply:** keep choices separate between accounts and away from public-site
branding. Be explicit about browser-only persistence until account syncing is added.
