---
name: Public website color palette
description: Shared color direction for Atlas Ingredients' public website and storefront, including boundaries with portal themes.
---

Use one shared botanical palette across public-facing website routes: forest green, sage, and warm paper/cream neutrals. Keep authenticated CRM and customer portal appearance settings separate, and preserve colors that carry certification, product-category, or status meaning.

**Why:** the owner asked for the same color palette across the website frontend, while portal colors are personal settings and certification/product colors can communicate identity.

**How to apply:** use the shared `--atlas-*` tokens and the public-theme scope on visitor-facing pages. Do not apply it to account-themed portal pages, and avoid changing meaningful data/status colors just to make every pixel identical.
