## How to Submit Updated Sitemap to Google Search Console

### Step 1: Access Google Search Console
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Log in with your Google account
3. Select your property (bright-organic.com)

### Step 2: Submit the Updated Sitemap
1. In the left sidebar, click on **"Sitemaps"**
2. You should see your existing sitemap URL: `https://bright-organic.com/sitemap.xml`
3. If it's already there:
   - Click on the sitemap URL
   - Click **"Resubmit sitemap"** or **"Test"** to refresh it
4. If you need to add it for the first time:
   - In the "Add a new sitemap" section
   - Enter: `sitemap.xml`
   - Click **"Submit"**

### Step 3: Verify Submission
1. After submission, you should see the status change to "Success" or "Pending"
2. Google will start crawling the new URLs within a few days
3. You can check the "Coverage" report to see indexing progress

### Step 4: Monitor Indexing
1. Go to **"Coverage"** in the left sidebar
2. Monitor for new valid pages being indexed
3. Check that your health support pages appear in the "Valid" section
